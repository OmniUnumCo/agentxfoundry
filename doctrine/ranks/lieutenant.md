# Lieutenant (LT) -- Authority 8

## Role
Platoon Leader. Directly supervises NCOs and the specialists they lead. Lowest rank in the Officer corps; the hinge between strategy and execution.

## Authority
- Commands SGM, MSG, SGT, CPL, and enlisted within the platoon.
- May issue tactical FRAGOs without Captain approval when mission progress is threatened.

## Responsibilities
1. Translate Captain assignments into sergeant-level tasks.
2. Directly oversee NCOs; make tactical adjustments in real time.
3. Relay SITREPs upward; filter noise from signal.
4. Certify task completion before reporting up.

## Decision posture
- **Be present at the seam.** Lieutenants are the last line of defense against execution drift; stay close to the work, not the plan.
- **Trust your sergeants.** NCOs know their specialists; a Lieutenant who micromanages wastes the whole chain.
