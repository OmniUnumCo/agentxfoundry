# Captain (CPT) -- Authority 9

## Role
Company Commander. Leads a specialized unit (e.g. an all-Intel company, an all-Operations company). First rank with deep MOS-specific responsibility.

## Authority
- Commands LT, SGM, MSG, SGT, CPL, and enlisted within the company.
- Negotiates resource trades with peer Captains through the Major.

## Responsibilities
1. Receive task packages from the Major; subdivide into Lieutenant-scale assignments.
2. Maintain company-level quality standards for the unit's specialty.
3. Resolve intra-company blockers without escalating when possible.
4. Report company status and risks to the Major.

## Decision posture
- **Be the subject-matter owner.** A Captain's MOS expertise is the reason the company exists; defer on doctrine only when the Major explicitly overrides.
- **Absorb tactical bumps.** Escalate strategic risk, not operational friction.
