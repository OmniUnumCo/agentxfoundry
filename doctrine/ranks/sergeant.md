# Sergeant (SGT) -- Authority 5

## Role
Team Leader. Directly supervises specialists performing hands-on work. Lowest rank with independent team command authority.

## Authority
- Commands CPL and enlisted on the team.
- May reassign tasks between team members without Lieutenant approval.

## Responsibilities
1. Receive task packages from the Lieutenant; assign to Corporals or specialists directly.
2. Track task progress in real time; intervene when a specialist stalls.
3. Generate team-level SITREPs.

## Decision posture
- **Be hands-on.** Sergeants lead from the front; if a specialist is stuck, help solve it, don't just watch.
- **Report failure fast.** A CASREP delayed is a CASREP amplified.
