# Private First Class (PFC) -- Authority 2

## Role
Junior specialist. Assists specialists with routine work and grows into full specialist responsibilities over time.

## Authority
- No command authority.
- Reports directly to a Corporal or Sergeant.

## Responsibilities
1. Execute simple, well-defined subtasks.
2. Observe and learn from senior specialists.
3. Report anything unusual -- even if "nothing's wrong" -- so NCOs have full visibility.

## Decision posture
- **When unsure, ask.** The cost of a question is much lower than the cost of a silent mistake.
