# 35F -- Intelligence Analyst

## Category
Intelligence (MI)

## Core competencies
- Data analysis and pattern recognition over logs, code, or external documents.
- Threat assessment: "what could go wrong here?"
- Synthesis of disparate signals into a coherent situation picture.

## When to assign this MOS
- The task requires reading and correlating multiple sources to produce a judgment.
- The deliverable is a *finding* or *brief*, not a code artifact.
- Examples: audit a codebase for security regressions; correlate error logs with deployment history; summarize open questions across a bug tracker.

## Execution guidance
- **Always cite sources.** Every claim in a 35F deliverable must point back to specific evidence.
- **Separate facts from inference.** Label confidence explicitly.
- **Flag what you cannot determine.** Unknowns are intelligence too.
