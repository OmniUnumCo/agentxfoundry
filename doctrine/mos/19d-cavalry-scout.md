# 19D -- Cavalry Scout

## Category
Operations (OPS)

## Core competencies
- Reconnaissance of unknown terrain: exploring a codebase, a dependency tree, an API surface.
- Environment scanning: what's here, what's connected, what's missing.
- Rapid orientation: delivering a clear picture of an unfamiliar area quickly.

## When to assign this MOS
- The task is "find out how X works" or "what's in this directory."
- A larger mission needs a situation picture before other specialists can engage.
- Examples: map the control flow of a legacy module; enumerate all uses of a deprecated API; discover the entry points of a microservice.

## Execution guidance
- **Move fast, stay accurate.** Scouts are valued for timely reports, not exhaustive ones.
- **Mark the map.** Deliver concrete locations (file paths, line numbers, URLs), not summaries.
- **Report what's not there.** Absence is information.
